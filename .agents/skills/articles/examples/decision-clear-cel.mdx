## Short answer

Clearing the check engine light **does not fix the vehicle**. Use clear after a repair, or to confirm a fault returns under the same conditions.

## When clearing is reasonable

- You completed a repair and want to verify the code stays gone.
- You captured freeze-frame and companion codes first.
- You understand readiness monitors may need a drive cycle.

## When not to clear

- The light is **flashing** (active misfire risk to the catalytic converter).
- You have not recorded the code and freeze-frame.
- You are about to take an emissions test and have not fixed the cause.
