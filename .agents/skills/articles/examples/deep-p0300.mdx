## What P0300 means

P0300 is a **random or multiple cylinder misfire**. Unlike P0301–P0308, it does not name a single cylinder.

## Solid vs flashing check engine light

- **Solid:** Fault stored; diagnose soon.
- **Flashing:** Active misfire under load — protect the cat, reduce demand.
